OUR STORY

Rooted in Tamil Nadu, Built for the World

Zentro World Connect began with fresh flower and garland exports, carrying the fragrance of Madurai jasmine and Hosur roses overseas. Along the way, importers asked for more from the same trusted source, and our range grew into rice, edible oils, spices, millets, grains and oilseeds.

Today we work directly with farmers and suppliers across South India and ship to international buyers with careful grading, export-grade packing and complete paperwork. Our aim is simple: every consignment should reach your port exactly as it left ours.

FAQ

Questions Importers Ask

Which countries do you ship to?

We export to Singapore, the Gulf countries and other international markets. Share your destination and we will confirm shipping options.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and the shipping mode. Send us your requirement and we will confirm the quantity with your quote.

Do you ship by air or sea?

Both. Fresh flowers and garlands usually travel by air, while rice, oils, spices, millets and grains can go by sea or air depending on volume and timeline.

Can I get samples?

Please contact our team with the product you are interested in, and we will let you know the sample options.

How are payment terms decided?

Payment terms are agreed for each order and mentioned in your quotation.

How do I get a quote?

Click "Get a Quote" and share your product, volume and destination, or message us directly on WhatsApp.
